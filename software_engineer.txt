Requirements

Bachelor's degree in computer science, Information Technology, Software Engineering, or a related field. 
Fresh graduates are welcome to apply. 
Applicants must be available for full-time employment. Undergraduate students who are still attending regular classes are not eligible. 
Proficiency in C#/.NET and Java (mandatory). 
Fluent in English, both written and verbal, is a must. 
Experience with one or more frontend/backend frameworks such as Blazor, React.js, Vue.js, TypeScript, or Spring Framework is an advantage. 
Knowledge of relational databases, particularly MySQL and Oracle Database. 
Familiarity with Figma and the ability to collaborate with UI/UX designers to implement user interface designs.
