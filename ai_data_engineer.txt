## Job Description – AI & Data Engineer

We are looking for an **AI & Data Engineer** to design, build, and maintain data-driven solutions that improve business operations and decision-making. 
The role involves developing backend applications, building data pipelines, deploying AI/ML solutions, and creating analytics dashboards.

### Required Skills
* **Flask**
* **Machine Learning**

### Preferred Qualifications
* Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or a related field.
* Experience delivering AI and data engineering solutions in production environments.
