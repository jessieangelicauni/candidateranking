## Job Description – AI & Data Engineer

We are looking for an **AI & Data Engineer** to design, build, and maintain data-driven solutions that improve business operations and decision-making. The role involves developing backend applications, building data pipelines, deploying AI/ML solutions, and creating analytics dashboards.

### Key Responsibilities

* Develop backend applications and REST APIs using **Python**, **FastAPI**, and **Flask**.
* Design and maintain **ETL pipelines** for processing structured and unstructured data.
* Build and optimize **SQL** databases and data models.
* Develop and deploy **AI/ML** solutions, including **LLM-powered applications**, predictive models, and computer vision systems.
* Build analytics dashboards and reports using **Power BI**.
* Deploy and manage applications on **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**.
* Integrate AI models into production systems and business workflows.
* Analyze operational data to improve performance and support business decisions.

### Required Skills

* **Python**
* **SQL**
* **FastAPI**, **Flask**, **REST APIs**
* **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)**
* **ETL Pipeline Development**
* **Power BI** and **DAX**
* **Machine Learning**
* **Large Language Models (LLMs)** and Prompt Engineering
* **Predictive Modeling**
* **Computer Vision**
* **Time-Series Forecasting**
* Data Modeling

### Preferred Qualifications

* Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or a related field.
* Experience delivering AI and data engineering solutions in production environments.
